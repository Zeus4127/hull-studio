Services

General practice

GP appointments

Standard and long visits with one of our doctors for everyday health concerns.

  • Standard and long appointments
  • Some same-day times each morning
  • Book online or by phone

Skin checks

A check of your skin by a doctor, with a note kept in your file.

  • Book a longer appointment
  • Bring a list of any spots you are worried about
  • Next steps are talked through with you

Health assessments

A regular check-up to look at your health history and plan what to do next.

  • Time to talk through your history
  • Medicare rebate details explained when you book
  • Your records kept in one place

Women's health

Appointments for women's health needs, with plenty of time and privacy.

  • Cervical screening and other routine checks
  • Time to talk in private
  • Ask for a female doctor when you book

Chronic care plans

Regular visits and planning for long-term health conditions, with your doctor and care team.

  • Care plans set up with your doctor
  • Reviews booked ahead
  • Referrals to other services when needed

Vaccinations

Vaccines for children, adults and travellers, given by our nurses by appointment.

  • Childhood and school vaccines
  • Flu vaccines in season
  • Travel vaccine advice booked ahead
