About Mallow Creek

A local yard, with the price on the windscreen.

Mallow Creek Motors started in 2012 with one yard in Warwick. Today two yards sell used utes, 4x4s, cars, light trucks and farm machinery, and our own workshop looks over every vehicle before it goes on the lot.

  • Every vehicle is checked in our own workshop before sale.
  • The price, kilometres and any price drop are shown on the listing.
  • You can ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection.
