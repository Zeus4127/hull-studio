About Wattlebrook

A friendly practice, close to home.

Wattlebrook Medical Centre opened in 2015 to give Warwick families a general practice they can book easily. Our doctors, nurses and reception team look after patients of all ages in one place.

  • Appointments by phone or online.
  • Clear information about fees before you book.
  • Time to ask questions in every visit.

Questions

Answers, straight up.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online or call (07) 5550 0381 during opening hours. Tell us if it is your first visit so we can set aside enough time.

Do you bulk bill?

Fees and bulk billing details are listed here. Ask our reception team what applies to you when you book.

What should I bring?

Your Medicare card, any concession or Health Care card, a list of your medicines, and any letters or test results you have.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, and Saturday mornings, 8am to 12pm. We are closed on public holidays.

Call us or book online. We will tell you what to bring.
