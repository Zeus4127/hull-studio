What we do

Services

Wines and cellar door

Cellar door tastings

Taste five or six wines at our timber bar, with a plate of local cheese if you like.

  • Open daily, 10am to 4pm
  • Groups of eight or more, please book
  • The tasting fee comes off any bottles you buy

Wine club

Four boxes a year, picked by our winemaker and sent to your door.

  • Choose reds, whites or a mix
  • Member prices on everything we make
  • Skip or pause a box any time

Online shop

Order single bottles or cases and have them sent anywhere in Australia.

  • Free freight on larger orders
  • Adult signature on delivery
  • Gift packs and cards available

Functions and weddings

Hold your wedding, birthday or work lunch in our barn or on the lawn.

  • The barn seats up to 80 people
  • Catering by local suppliers
  • Our team helps plan the day

Vineyard tours

Walk the rows with the person who looks after the vines, then taste what they make.

  • Tours run on weekends and school holidays
  • About 90 minutes, easy walking
  • A tasting at the end

Wholesale

We supply restaurants, bottle shops and cafes across south-east Queensland.

  • Case and pallet orders
  • Regular delivery runs
  • Samples for new stockists

Plan your visit.

Tell us the day and how many are coming. We will reply the same day.
