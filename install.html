Download for Android

  1. Tap the download button and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open Studio Hull from your home screen.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Studio Hull now opens like an app, and works without a signal.
