Privacy

This page explains what Keystone Web Studio does with personal information collected through this website. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, company, phone number, email address and the details of your job, when you use the quote form, email us or call us.

How we use it

To answer your request, prepare a quote and carry out the work. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

The quote form

The form sends your details to our inbox by email. If that is not possible, it opens your own email app with the details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. To work without a signal, it saves copies of its own pages on your device. You can clear these in your browser settings at any time.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or make a complaint, by contacting us at hello@example.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
