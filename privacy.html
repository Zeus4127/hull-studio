Privacy

This page explains what Studio Hull does with personal information collected through this website and app. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, company, email address, website address and the details of your job, when you use the quote form.

How we use it

To answer your request, prepare a quote and carry out the work. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

The forms

Our email address is being set up. Until then the forms cannot send anything. On the Start a project page you can save your answers as a file on your own device. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send that file to us.

The website check

We check your website by hand. You send us the address of your site and an email address to send the report to. We use them only to check the site and send you the report. No automatic test runs on the page, and your address is not sent to any other company.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. To work without a signal, it saves copies of its own pages on your device. You can clear these in your browser settings at any time.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or make a complaint, through the contact page once our email address is set up. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
